# GENZI Reporting

Users can report a published post from the Mini App. Reports are stored locally in SQLite and visible to authenticated owner/moderator accounts.

Supported reasons:
- SPAM
- SCAM
- HARASSMENT
- SEXUAL_CONTENT
- NON_CONSENSUAL_INTIMATE_CONTENT
- DOXXING
- THREATS
- HATE
- DANGEROUS_CONTENT
- OTHER

Reports are not automatically treated as proof of a violation. Moderators review the report and may dismiss it or remove the reported post.
